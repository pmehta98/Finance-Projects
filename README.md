## 📌 Overview

This repository contains three comprehensive projects that explore diverse aspects of **finance and economics**. These projects were created as part of academic coursework and delve into critical topics including derivatives trading, comparative economic development, and mutual fund analysis.

Each project combines theoretical frameworks with practical analysis, providing actionable insights into complex financial and economic phenomena.

---

## 📁 Projects

### 📈 Derivatives and Risk Management

- **Folder:** [`Derivatives and Risk Management`](./Derivatives%20and%20Risk%20Management)
- **Objective:** Analyze the impact of leverage on financial instruments using TVS Motors as the underlying asset
- **Analysis Period:** Two periods during the COVID-19 pandemic
- **Instruments Analyzed:**
  - Treasury Bills (T-Bills)
  - Fixed Deposits
  - Stocks
  - Futures
  - Options
- **Key Insights:**
  - Safer instruments like T-Bills are better for risk-averse investors
  - Derivatives can offer higher returns for those with higher risk tolerance
  - Leverage amplifies both gains and losses
  - Understanding risk-return tradeoffs is critical for portfolio construction

[Learn more about this project](./Derivatives%20and%20Risk%20Management/README.md)

### 🌍 Development Economics

- **Folder:** [`Dev Eco`](./Dev%20Eco)
- **Objective:** Compare the economic development of three coastal and three landlocked countries
- **Key Indicators Analyzed:**
  - Gross Domestic Product (GDP)
  - Employment distribution across sectors
  - Education expenditure
  - Trade patterns and dependencies
- **Countries Studied:**
  - Coastal nations (with natural trade advantages)
  - Landlocked nations (facing geographical constraints)
- **Key Insights:**
  - Coastal nations benefit from direct access to international trade routes
  - Landlocked nations face higher transport costs and increased dependency on neighbors
  - Geography plays a significant role in economic development trajectories
  - Policy interventions can help mitigate geographical disadvantages

[Learn more about this project](./Dev%20Eco/README.md)

---

### 📊 Security Analysis and Portfolio Management

- **Folder:** [`Security Analysis and Portfolio Management`](./Security%20Analysis%20and%20Portfolio%20Management)
- **Objective:** Evaluate the market timing ability of small-cap mutual funds using regression methodologies
- **Methodology:**
  - Regression analysis
  - Performance evaluation across multiple time horizons
  - Comparative ranking of funds
- **Scope:**
  - 12 small-cap mutual funds analyzed
  - Investment windows: 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year periods
- **Key Insights:**
  - Market timing is challenging in small-cap funds
  - Limited consistent success over longer periods
  - Performance persistence is rare in small-cap segment
  - Passive strategies may be more reliable for small-cap exposure

[Learn more about this project](./Security%20Analysis%20and%20Portfolio%20Management/README.md)

## 🛠️ Setup

### Prerequisites

- Python 3.8 or higher (for data analysis projects)
- Excel or Google Sheets (for viewing data files)
- R or RStudio (if applicable for statistical analysis)

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash

   cd Finance-Projects
   ```

2. **Navigate to a specific project folder:**
